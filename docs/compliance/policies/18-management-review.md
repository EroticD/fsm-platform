# POL-18 Management Review

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Policy ID | POL-18 |
| Version | 0.1 readiness draft |
| Owner | Managing Director |
| Approver | Managing Director |
| Review cadence | Annual, plus a mid-year checkpoint |
| Scope | ISMS performance, risk, incidents, audit, suppliers, privacy, resources and improvement |
| Related ISO Annex A | 5.4, 5.36 |
| Related SOC 2 | CC1.2, CC4, CC5.3 |
| Evidence | Agenda, minutes, actions, KPI pack, decisions |

## 1. Purpose

Ensure top management reviews the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the
ISMS and takes decisions on risk, resources and improvement.

## 2. Inputs

The review must consider:

- status of actions from the previous review;
- changes in scope, law, contracts, suppliers, architecture and threats;
- risk register, risk appetite and treatment progress;
- incidents, near misses, breaches and notifications;
- internal audit findings and corrective actions;
- vulnerability and patch status;
- access reviews and privileged access exceptions;
- backup/restore and DR exercise results;
- supplier attestations, DPAs and subprocessor changes;
- privacy programme, DSRs, DPIAs and retention decisions;
- training completion and awareness;
- customer complaints, questionnaires and contractual commitments;
- audit/certification status and evidence gaps;
- resource needs and budget;
- opportunities for improvement.

## 3. Outputs

Minutes must record decisions on:

- risk treatment and acceptance (including residual risk above appetite);
- policy/SoA changes;
- resource and budget allocation;
- corrective actions with owner and due date;
- audit and certification readiness;
- changes to scope or customer commitments;
- training and staffing needs;
- any matter escalated to the board/owners.

## 4. Agenda template

1. Purpose, attendees, conflicts of interest.
2. Previous actions.
3. Business/scope changes.
4. Risk profile and treatments.
5. Incidents and privacy.
6. Audit findings and compliance.
7. Operations, availability and backups.
8. Suppliers and subprocessors.
9. Access and security metrics.
10. Training and awareness.
11. Customer/contract commitments.
12. Resources and budget.
13. Decisions and actions.
14. Next review.

## 5. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Managing Director | Chairs, decides, owns resources and risk acceptance. |
| Security Lead | Prepares the KPI pack and risk/audit status. |
| Operations Lead | Reports availability, backups, incidents and capacity. |
| Privacy Officer | Reports DSRs, DPIAs, retention and supplier privacy. |
| Internal Auditor | Presents independent findings. |
| Action owners | Report progress and evidence. |

## 6. KPIs

| KPI | Target | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical/High risks above appetite | 0 or approved | Risk register |
| Overdue risk treatments | 0 | Risk register |
| SEV-1/2 incidents | Trend; all reviewed | Incident register |
| Access reviews completed | 100% | Access review |
| Critical vulnerabilities past due | 0 | Vulnerability register |
| Backup restore tests passed | 100% | Restore log |
| DR exercise completed | Annual | Exercise report |
| Supplier attestations/DPAs current | 100% | Supplier register |
| DSRs answered on time | 100% | Request log |
| Training completion | 100% | Training register |
| Internal audit actions closed on time | ≥ 90% | Action log |
| Policy reviews current | 100% | Policy index |

## 7. Minutes template

Use [templates/MANAGEMENT-REVIEW.md](../templates/MANAGEMENT-REVIEW.md). The
minutes must be specific enough that a reader can tell what was decided, by
whom, by when and with what evidence. "Security reviewed" is not a decision.

## 8. Evidence

- Agenda and attendance.
- KPI pack and risk report.
- Minutes with decisions and actions.
- Action tracker with closure evidence.
- Updated risk register, policies and SoA where changed.
