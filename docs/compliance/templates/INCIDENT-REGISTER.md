## Severity quick guide

| Severity | Example | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV-1 | Confirmed breach, production compromise, ransomware, extended outage | Immediate escalation and containment |
| SEV-2 | Credential compromise, single-customer exposure, significant degradation | Same-day containment |
| SEV-3 | Failed attack, limited exposure, low-impact vulnerability | Next business day |
| SEV-4 | Suspicious activity, policy question | Within 3 business days |
